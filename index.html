PROJECT 01 · P5.JS

Sounds of Discomfort

My project explores how ordinary sounds can create discomfort, tension, and anxiety. Inspired by chalk scratching on a blackboard, Styrofoam rubbing, mosquito buzzing, alarm clocks, and construction drills, I built five interactive scenes using p5.js. Visitors drag the mouse to scratch chalk or rub foam, chase a mosquito that escapes their cursor, press the space bar to toggle an alarm, and hold the mouse to activate a drill. Rather than making something pleasant, I wanted people to notice how pitch, repetition, and frequency influence our emotions and bodies. The project turns everyday irritation into an interactive audiovisual experience for visitors.

PROJECT 02 · HYPERNARRATIVE

My Suitcase

My Suitcase began with the experience of packing before studying abroad. I realized that a suitcase carries more than clothes: it also holds memories, emotions, and pieces of identity. In this interactive self-portrait, visitors explore objects such as dance shoes, diaries, toys, and plane tickets, each connected to a different stage of my life. They can make choices while moving through the stories, reflecting how growing up often means keeping something and leaving something else behind. Through this project, I explore how childhood, home, and personal experiences continue to shape us, even when we begin a new life far away.
