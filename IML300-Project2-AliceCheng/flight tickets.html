A Journey Far From Home

A story about leaving home and growing up.

You have always wondered what life would be like somewhere far away.

One day, you receive an opportunity to study abroad.

Leaving means discovering a new world, meeting new people, and becoming more independent.

But it also means saying goodbye to your family, your hometown, and the familiar taste of the fruits you grew up eating.

You look at the ticket in your hand.

Will you take the flight, or choose to stay?
