Where I Grew Up

Memories of Yantai

I grew up in Yantai, a city by the sea. The ocean has always been part of my life. The sound of the waves, the smell of the sea, and the sunsets by the water are all familiar memories. No matter how far I travel, the sea will always remind me of home.

The sea connects who I was with who I am.
