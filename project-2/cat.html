The Cat I Wanted to Keep

A story about love, responsibility, and standing up for what you want.

You have always loved cats. One day, you finally ask your mom if you can have one. But she says no. She worries about the mess, the cost, and who will take care of it. You promise that you will be responsible, but she still isn't convinced.

Will you keep trying to change her mind, or let go of your wish?
