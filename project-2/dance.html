The Shoes I Almost Left Behind

A story about growing up and finding my passion.

You started dancing when you were four. At first, dancing was something you loved.

But as you grew older, endless practices, competitions, and expectations slowly made dancing feel like a responsibility.

One day, while packing your suitcase, you find your old dance shoes. They remind you of both the happiness and the pressure.

What will you do with them?
